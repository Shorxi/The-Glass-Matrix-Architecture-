# Open Hardware & Open Silicon Carrier Declaration

This project is released as an **open hardware and open interconnect platform** for the
global semiconductor ecosystem.

## Scope

You are explicitly allowed to:

- Manufacture MVC wafers and Solid-Flow carriers according to these specifications.
- Integrate any third-party silicon dies (CPU, GPU, NPU, ASIC, etc.).
- Design your own mainboards, sockets, and external cooling loops.
- Use the platform in commercial and non-commercial products.

You are required to:

- Respect the licenses listed in `LICENSES/` (AGPL-3.0 and CERN-OHL-S).
- Preserve attribution to the original author.
- Publish modifications to the carrier architecture and interconnect specifications under
  compatible open licenses, where applicable.

## Non-Scope

This repository does **not** include:

- Proprietary silicon designs.
- Proprietary top-cooler designs.
- Any closed-source firmware or software.

The carrier is a **universal, passive, open platform**. Active logic remains under the control
of each vendor.
