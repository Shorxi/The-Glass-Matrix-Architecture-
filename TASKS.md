# Implementation Task List (For Industry Partners)

## Task 1 – Material & Wafer Prototyping
- Reproduce the MVC glass composition (4.5% passivated Al-Mg, 15 nm Al2O3 shell).
- Validate mechanical properties (E_eff, K_IC) and thermal conductivity.
- Produce first 300 mm wafers at target thickness (thin MVC and/or 6.0 mm Solid-Flow).

## Task 2 – Channel & Manifold Fabrication
- Implement IR-based DLMS or SLE for:
  - Macroscopic 3.2–4.0 mm channels (Solid-Flow).
  - Internal cross-mesh U-turn manifold.
- Verify hermetic sealing of external ports after drilling/etching.

## Task 3 – Interconnect Stack
- Deposit Ti/Cu/Ni/Au UBM stack on bottom TGVs.
- Implement AJP silver + UV planarization for data vias.
- Validate impedance, S-parameters, and high-frequency behavior.

## Task 4 – Carrier-to-Board Integration
- Design a reference PCB with:
  - Matched pad capacitance (Z_0 ~ 50 Ω).
  - Fluidic manifold and O-ring interfaces.
  - Optional Co2Z inductive control bus.

## Task 5 – Silicon Integration
- Mount a reference die (CPU/GPU/NPU) via ACF.
- Apply top-side cooling (air, liquid, or phase-change).
- Run thermal and performance benchmarks vs. classical interposers.

## Task 6 – Open Publication
- Publish measurement data, deviations, and improvements.
- Contribute back updated dimensions, tolerances, and process notes.
- Maintain compatibility with the base open specification.
